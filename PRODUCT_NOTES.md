# CoBest product direction

CoBest should be presented as a real website and commerce product, not as a showcase or sample. Public pages focus on outcomes, pricing, trust, and starting a store. The merchant application uses real empty states for new accounts instead of invented sales, orders, customers, analytics, or campaigns.

## Core position

**Build a store people trust. Run it from one place.**

CoBest combines business discovery, website building, content, and commerce. The key distinction is that setup begins with the business rather than a blank template.

## Launch requirement

Before charging customers, production authentication, persistent storage, payment processing, checkout, and transactional email must be connected and tested.
